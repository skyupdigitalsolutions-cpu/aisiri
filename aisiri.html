About the Chamber

Built on trust.
Driven by results.

Aisiri Law Chambers is a general practice legal firm based in Bengaluru, Karnataka. We represent clients across criminal, civil, corporate, and family matters — with a client-first philosophy that prioritises honesty, clarity, and strategic thinking.

We believe access to quality legal counsel should feel straightforward, not intimidating. From your first call to final order, we are with you every step of the way.

What We Do

Areas of practice.

We appear before District Courts, Sessions Courts, and the Karnataka High Court in the following areas of law.

01

Criminal Defense

Bail applications, anticipatory bail, trial defense, appeals, and representation in sessions and High Court criminal matters.

02

Civil Litigation

Suits for recovery, injunctions, specific performance, declaratory reliefs, and civil appeals before courts.

03

Property Disputes

Title disputes, partition suits, encroachment matters, land acquisition challenges, and RERA complaints.

04

Family Law

Divorce, maintenance, child custody, domestic violence, guardianship, and matrimonial property disputes.

05

Corporate & Commercial

Contract review, business disputes, cheque dishonour (NI Act), debt recovery, and commercial agreements.

06

Writ & Constitutional

Writ petitions before the Karnataka High Court for fundamental rights, service matters, and governmental challenges.
